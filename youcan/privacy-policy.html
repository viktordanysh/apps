YOU CAN.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

YOU CAN. is a habit and goal tracking app. The app does not require account creation and does not ask for profile, payment, contact, location, photo, or address book access.

Information Stored On Device

Habit titles, frequency, start date, progress, target count, and reminder time are intended to be stored locally on the user's device as part of the normal app experience.

AI Features and Google Gemini

AI chat and AI-generated reminder text are optional. If the user gives consent, chat messages and minimal active habit context may be sent to Google Gemini to generate a reply or reminder text. Inactive templates are not sent.

If consent is not provided, the app does not send this content to Gemini and uses local reminder text instead.

Tracking and Data Sharing

YOU CAN. does not sell personal information, does not use advertising tracking, and does not track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.

User Choices

Users can use the app without AI features and can withdraw AI consent inside the app. Users should not enter sensitive personal, medical, financial, or confidential information in the AI chat.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the support details listed for YOU CAN. on the App Store.
